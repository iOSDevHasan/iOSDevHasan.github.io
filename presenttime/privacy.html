PresentTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

PresentTime (“the app”) is an Apple Watch presentation timer developed by Hasan Berat Gürbüz. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Data We Collect

PresentTime does not collect personal data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to a server operated by the developer.

Crash Reports

To improve stability, PresentTime uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends technical diagnostics: the stack trace, app version, device model, watchOS version, a random installation identifier and the timer context at that moment, such as the selected duration. Crash reports are not linked to your identity and are not used for tracking or advertising. Google processes this data under the Firebase terms and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves only your preferences on your device, such as your last selected duration and whether you have seen the introduction. This information is removed if you delete the app.

Notifications

PresentTime can schedule local notifications to alert you as your presentation time runs out. They are created on your device and are not sent through a developer-operated server.

Purchases

PresentTime is a paid app sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details.

Children’s Privacy

PresentTime does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact Hasan Berat Gürbüz at hasan.gurbuz11@hotmail.com.
