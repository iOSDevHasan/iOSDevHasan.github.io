PresentTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

PresentTime is designed with your privacy in mind. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Data We Collect

PresentTime does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not connect to a developer-operated server; nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves only your preferences locally on your device, such as your last selected duration and whether you have seen the introduction. This information never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Notifications

PresentTime may schedule local notifications on your device to alert you as your presentation time runs out. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not sent through an external service operated by us.

Children's Privacy

PresentTime does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact
