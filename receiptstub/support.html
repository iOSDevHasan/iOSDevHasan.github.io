Quick checks

  • For a long receipt, place only the section you want to read inside the fixed scan window.
  • If the receipt is blurry or reflective, move closer, hold steady, and tilt away from glare.
  • Use Restore Purchases on the purchase screen if you previously bought Full Access with the same Apple Account.
  • Receipt recognition can make mistakes; review extracted values before relying on them as a financial record.
